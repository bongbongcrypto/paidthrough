## 3. FSB 2025 progress report: retail/P2P cross-border payments within one hour (3.5%?) and within a day (44.3%?); G20 remittance cost target 3% by 2030

Status: PARTLY. 3.5% and 44.3% are the B2P (business-to-person) column, not retail overall and not P2P. The 3% by 2030 target is confirmed, but it comes from the 2021 targets document.

Figure as published (FSB 2025 KPIs; payment speed data from FXC Intelligence as of March 2025, remittance data from RPW Q1 2025):
- Retail overall (Table 11): within one hour 35.4%; within one business day 67.3%
- B2P (Table 12): within one hour 3.5%; within one business day 44.3%
- P2P (Table 12): within one hour 46.4%; within one business day 75.9%
- Remittances (Table 17): within one hour 54.4%; within one business day 76.3%
- Remittance cost (Table 16): global average cost of sending USD 200 = 6.5%; USD 500 = 4.3%
- Target: global average cost of sending $200 remittance no more than 3% by 2030; no corridor above 5%

Source:
- "G20 Roadmap for Enhancing Cross-border Payments – Consolidated progress report for 2025", Financial Stability Board, 9 October 2025. https://www.fsb.org/uploads/P091025-1.pdf (Annex tables 11, 12, 16, 17, pp.53–57)
- The companion methodology document, "Annual Progress Report on Meeting the Targets for Cross-Border Payments – Methodology document, 2025 update", FSB, 9 October 2025, is at https://www.fsb.org/uploads/P091025-2.pdf
- Target: "Targets for Addressing the Four Challenges of Cross-Border Payments: Final Report", FSB, 13 October 2021, Table 1. https://www.fsb.org/uploads/P131021-2.pdf

Unit: every speed KPI counts the share of payment services (by PSP), not the share of payments or of value. In the FSB framework, retail P2P means non-remittance P2P (the methodology document's label is "P2P (non-remittances)"). Remittances are a separate segment (Table 17).

Quote:
- Table 11 row label: "Percentage of cross-border retail payments services (by PSP) that credit recipients within one hour of initiation", 2025 = 35.4%
- Table 17 row label: "Percentage of services making remittance funds available to the recipient within one hour", 2025 = 54.4%. Table note: "Based on sending USD 200."
- Table 12 row "KPI 1: within one hour of initiation", B2P 2025 = 3.5%; P2P 2025 = 46.4%
- Table 12 row "KPI 2: within one business day of initiation", B2P 2025 = 44.3%; P2P 2025 = 75.9%
- 2025 report, §3.3: "The global share of remittance services making funds available to the recipient within one hour increased to 54.4%"
- 2025 report, §3.3: "The global average cost for sending USD 200 and USD 500 remittances remained stable relative to the 2024 values (at 6.5% and 4.3%, respectively)"
- 2021 targets, Table 1 (Remittances, "Reaffirm UN SDG"): "Global average cost of sending $200 remittance to be no more than 3% by 2030, with no corridors with costs higher than 5%"

## 9. Korea: taking KRW and delivering money abroad without registration is an illegal foreign-exchange business (환치기, Foreign Exchange Transactions Act)

Status: CONFIRMED

Figure as published: Running a foreign exchange business requires prior registration (Article 8(1)). The penalty for violation is up to 3 years' imprisonment or a fine of up to 300 million won (외국환거래법 제27조의2), per the Korean government source below.

Source:
- Foreign Exchange Transactions Act, English/Korean parallel text, Korea Legislation Research Institute. https://elaw.klri.re.kr/kor_service/lawTwoView.do?hseq=38381 (the translation's version date was not verified)
- Ministry of Foreign Affairs, 주필리핀 대한민국 대사관 세부 분관, "'환전사기' 및 '불법외환거래' 관련 주의사항", posted 2019-05-10. https://overseas.mofa.go.kr/ph-cebu-ko/brd/m_2344/view.do?seq=1334031

Quote:
- Art. 8(1) (English): "Any person who intends to engage in a foreign exchange affair shall … register it as business with the Minister of Strategy and Finance"
- MOFA: "환치기란 무등록 외국환 업무로서 해외로 실제로 송금 행위는 이루어지 지 않으면서 국내 환치기업자의 계좌에 입금하면"
- MOFA: "외국환거래법 제27조의 2에 의해서 3년 이하의 징역 또는 3억원 이하의 벌금에 처해 집니다."

Note: The penalty is cited from MOFA only, because article numbering for penalties in the elaw translation came back inconsistent.
